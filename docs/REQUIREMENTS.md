# Medlink VA Website Requirements

## Business

Medlink VA is a virtual medical assistant business.

## Main Website Goals

1. Attract prospective clients.
2. Generate organic traffic.
3. Build trust and desire for Medlink VA services.
4. Convert visitors into leads and clients.
5. Capture visitor email addresses.
6. Support email marketing and lead growth.
7. Publish links to classes.
8. Publish job vacancies.
9. Promote products available through an external sales link.
10. Allow the client to update selected website content without technical work.

## Website Architecture

Initial version does not require a custom application database.

Planned technologies:

- React
- Vite
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Sanity CMS
- Brevo
- Cloudflare Pages
- GitHub

## Content Strategy

Some final business information is not yet available.

Professional draft copy may be created for:
- hero section
- About section
- service introductions
- benefits
- calls to action
- general FAQs

Do NOT fabricate factual claims such as:
- testimonials
- certifications
- HIPAA compliance
- number of clients
- years of experience
- awards
- partnerships
- staff qualifications

Missing factual information should remain clearly editable/placeheld for later client input.
